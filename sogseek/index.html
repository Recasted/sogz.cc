Private by design. sogz.cc never sees or stores your image. It goes straight from your browser to the engine you click, and that engine's own privacy rules apply.

Tip: crop to the part you care about (one panel, one character, no borders or captions). Engines match much better on a clean crop.

Not included: face-recognition engines that identify a person from their face. SogSeek finds sources, not people.
