Approve player comments

  1. Open your Google Form, click Responses, then the green Sheets icon.
  2. Select the new rows (click the row numbers on the left, without the header row) and copy them (Ctrl+C).
  3. Paste them in the box below (Ctrl+V) and click Read rows.
  4. Tick the comments you want to keep. Comments the filter thinks are hateful are marked red and start unticked.
  5. Click Download new approved-comments.js. On GitHub, open angel-exe/shared/, click Add file > Upload files, drop the downloaded file in and click Commit changes.
