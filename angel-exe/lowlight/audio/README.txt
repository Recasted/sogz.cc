Put music files here ONLY if you own them or have permission to share them
(for example songs from a band you know who said yes, or your own music).

Then add "audio" to that song in data/music.js, for example:
  tracks: ["Song One", { title: "Song Two", audio: "audio/song-two.mp3" }]

.mp3 works in every browser. Songs without a file show "Listen on Spotify"
and "YouTube" buttons instead.
